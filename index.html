The Script · orange = what you say · green = optional

1 · OPEN + PROTECT TIME · first 3 min (≈80% of the call)
Icebreaker, quick and warm:
"Where are you calling from?"
If they launch into a pitch, interrupt gently:
"Hey [name], really appreciate you wanting to get right into it. Before we do, can I set the stage for how we best use this time? If it's okay with you, let's dive right in."
2 · ANCHOR TO THE AD
"You booked this call because you saw our ad. Was it the four founders one, or the case study one, basically a pay for performance team?"
Pause. Let them reconnect to it.
"What about the ad peaked your interest?"
Let them re-sell themselves.
3 · THE STORY (who we are)
"Let me tell you a little about me. I've founded 6 businesses and acquired 5, mostly in real estate, lending, and marketing. I met YAMU's founder Kanishka on an entrepreneurial reality TV show, of all things. I loved their performance model on net new revenue, so I brought my own businesses and my investors' companies to YAMU and they did great. Then Kanishka asked if I'd join the team, and here I am.
Other than Kanishka, 4 partners have done 150M+ in revenue and 4 exits, across product, tech, and services.
Because they're performance based, they're very specific about who they take on, so we have to qualify you before we can do an audit. My job is to ask a series of questions to make sure you're a fit. After this call we run a comprehensive audit: all ad platforms, GA4, Shopify or CRM, plus deep market, competitor, and customer research. It's more thorough than I ever expected to see.
We use it to have the confidence to take on performance clients. If we can't grow you, we don't profit, and we don't want to waste your time. Let me share my screen, can you see it?"
Let their eyes catch $150M+ combined revenue and exits, then the "work we do" slide.
"Once we engage we own the full revenue stack: CRO, email, creative, paid media, UGC, influencer. Because we're paid on revenue, we control everything that drives it. Two ways we engage, a Hybrid model and a Pure Performance model, and I'll get into both with numbers at the end. You good for us to roll into the questions?"
Name the models, hide the numbers. Hold the ecosystem and PE pitch, that's the home run saved for after questions.
4 · QUESTIONS · 6 to 10 min, 8 to 10 questions
"If you don't mind, I'll jump in and ask a number of key questions. Let me get through them, and if I missed anything you can fill the gaps at the end."
Never ask "tell me about your business." Ask narrow: how do you make money, who do you sell to, why do people choose you over a competitor. Ask the questions on the right and log the answers there. This call verifies what they put on the form.
5 · THE QUALIFIER · the last question
"Last one, how much are you spending on ad spend right now?"
Current ad spend is our proxy for product market fit. Frame it as proof, not a paywall: "We need enough spend history to have real conviction we can scale you." Set the answer in the Decision engine at the bottom right; it applies the rules and returns your path.

6 · ECOSYSTEM PITCH · the home run (after questions, before offer)
"Our founders have had four exits and done $150M+ for portfolio companies. Because we're confident in what we do, we take positions in companies, a private equity arm. We deploy cash and operational support. We did our first deal last year, a couple more in diligence, and we're even launching our own companies now."
This flips them from interviewing an agency to being evaluated by potential investors. Back the family office capacity ($20M, $1 to 5M per company) only if it fits.

8 · TRIAL CLOSE
"Based on what I shared, $7k base or the $12k one time, if we came back after the audit with one of these two, would that be within your range? You don't decide anything today. I just need to know it's workable before we put our team's time into the audit."
Log the answer in the Decision engine (trial close) so the call accounts for it.
9 · BOOK THE AUDIT + GET ACCESS
"Great. Let's get a call on the calendar 5 to 6 days out to walk through what we find. I'll need access to your Shopify, GA4, Meta, and Google. Who owns access on your side, and can we get them on a call?"

1 · OPEN · LEAD WITH THE GUARANTEE · 2 to 3 min
You're here to qualify, not sell. A "no" or "not yet" is a good outcome. The guarantee is the anchor of this call, lead with it. Everything flows from there.
"Before I tell you anything about the program, I want to start with our guarantee, because it's actually the reason this call exists."
"We have a 100% money-back guarantee written directly into our agreement. No forms, no deliberation. If at any point you don't find this valuable to your business, you send us a message and we refund you. That's it."
"Because we stand behind that guarantee completely, we're very intentional about who we accept. We don't want to fill a spot with a company we can't genuinely help, that's a bad experience for them and a wasted seat for someone who'd really benefit."
"This is a 90-day winning-ad bootcamp. We hand you the exact frameworks we use internally to launch campaigns that make our partners money, and we support you through the work to get there."
"So my job today is to make sure your business is the right fit. I'll ask you a series of questions, I'll be honest about what I find, then I'll walk you through the program so you can ask me anything. Sound good?"
"Which ad did you see, was it the founders one?"
The prospect should relax within 60 seconds. Don't jump into questions before the guarantee framing fully lands, or it doesn't work.
2 · DISCOVERY · 10 to 15 min
Business basics first, marketing second, economics third. Follow their answers, don't robotically tick a list. Ask the questions on the right and log the answers there.
"How long have you been running? What's your monthly revenue right now? What's your average sale or order value?"
"Where are your customers coming from, warm network, referrals, or cold sources?"
Most important question. All-warm-network revenue is real signal but behaves differently from cold. Zero cold customers is a flag to address head-on in Qualify.
"Have you run paid ads before, Meta or Google? Do you do any email marketing? What's your biggest challenge finding new customers?"
"What's your gross margin on a typical sale? Rough sense of what it costs to acquire a customer? Any repeat purchases? What's your refund rate?"
3 · QUALIFY · the decision point
Check three things, then say your read out loud: 1) Product-market fit (does the product work, and have cold strangers bought?) · 2) Unit economics (enough margin for ads to be profitable?) · 3) Time (5 to 10 hrs/week?). And quietly: do I like this person? Set these in the fit engine on the right.
Three stages: product works → cold traffic converts → scalable distribution. This program is for people who cleared stage 1 and are ready to prove stage 2.
If PMF is shaky: "What you have is real signal that your product works when you're in the room, that's genuinely not nothing. But cold traffic behaves differently, they're harder to convert and need more before they say yes. Before we run ads, you want a few cold customers who found you with no warm connection. That tells you the product can stand on its own."
4 · AUTHORITY · 1 to 2 min, light touch
"We run a performance marketing agency where we only get paid when our clients grow. We built this program because some of the best businesses we've seen weren't quite ready for that model yet, what they needed was to build the foundation first. Everything here is the exact process we use internally on real campaigns."
If relevant: "$150M+ built across our own companies, multiple exits, a Forbes 30 Under 30, and a top-15 Inc 5000 consumer brand."
One beat, then move on. Authority comes from the quality of your questions and the accuracy of your diagnosis, not from listing credentials.
5 · PROGRAM OVERVIEW · 8 to 10 min
"This is a bootcamp, not a course. You do the actual work. The 90-minute sessions aren't the magic, the homework is the gold. What we walk you through week by week is the exact process we use internally to build winning ad campaigns."
Lead with the research: "The first thing we do is a deep customer, competitor and market research report, 200 to 300 pages. Real quotes from Reddit, Quora, competitor reviews, forums, actual people talking about the problem and what they wish existed. That's voice-of-customer at a level most businesses never get, and it's what you write your ads to. Included, we run it for you at the start."
"We also build you a custom AI marketing strategist trained on your research. As you complete each week's homework, it assembles your campaign. You're building a real artifact, not busywork."
The arc (walk 2 to 3 weeks, don't read the menu): Wk 1-2 research + avatar · Wk 3 offer architecture · Wk 4 video script + 1:1 review · Wk 5 production brief (iPhone + $100-150 Fiverr editor) · Wk 6 landing page copy · Wk 7-8 launch (~$2k ad spend) · Wk 9-12 iterate and score.
Be upfront about homework: "The 90-minute sessions aren't magic. You're doing 5 to 10 hours of homework a week that builds the actual campaign, that's the real product. If you can't make that time, this won't work, and I'd rather tell you now than after you've paid."
Economics: "$2,500 a month for three months, $7,500 total, plus about $2,000 of your own ad spend in month three. A winning ad is one that's profitable after ad spend and cost of goods. Once you have it, you can dial the spend to $5k, $10k, $20k."
Key line: "You don't need to be a marketing expert. You need to be an expert at your own business."
6 · RE-ANCHOR THE GUARANTEE · before price questions
"I mentioned the guarantee at the top, to make it concrete: it's written directly into the agreement. If at any point you feel this isn't valuable to your business, you send us a message and we refund you. No forms, no case to make."
"This is also why we ask the questions we ask up front. We're taking on that financial risk, so we need to accept the right people. We're not filling seats, we're filling the right seats."
7 · THE ONLY REAL CATCH · TIME
"The financial risk is fully on us. The only thing you're putting at risk is your time, 5 to 10 hours a week for 12 weeks. If you have that, there's no other catch. And the real question is: is figuring out how to get customers scalably the most important thing your business needs right now? If yes, this is the time."
Watch for the prospect who says they have the time but clearly doesn't. Ask specifically: what does your week look like, do you have a team or is it mostly you?
8 · ONE-CALL CLOSE INCENTIVE
"If you put down a $1,000 deposit today, we cover your $2,000 in ad spend, a $2,000 discount. I'm not trying to be pushy, for us it's about locking in your spot without one more follow-up call. This cohort fills up and I've been told not to chase leads."
"And the deposit is refundable after Session 1. If you show up and it doesn't rock your world, we refund it."
If they genuinely need time: "That's totally fine. As soon as the deposit lands, I'll kick off your research that same day, you'll have 200-plus pages on your market before the cohort even starts."
Both decision-makers should ideally be on the call. If a partner needs to be looped in, surface that at the open, not at close time.
9 · CLOSE
"What questions do you have? And be honest, do you have any hesitations?"
Not rhetorical, wait for the real answer. Name the objection: cashflow, money, time, camera, or a missing stakeholder. Address the specific one, not a blanket reassurance.
If ready: "Can I send you the deposit link and agreement right now? I'll kick off your research today and send you a guide on how to use it."
If they want to see it first: "Let me send you the Session 1 recording, 90 minutes, you'll see the research depth and our full approach. Watch it and follow up in a couple of days. The deposit's even refundable after Session 1, so there's no risk to putting it down."
